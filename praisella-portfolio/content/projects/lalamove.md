## Problem

### A one-driver-per-vehicle model, looking for more supply.

Lalamove had built its last-mile model around individual drivers delivering for customers. The company needed new ways to grow the supply side, plus several product optimisations for smoother delivery.

## Solution

### Fleet: putting idle fleet capacity to work.

I initiated and launched the Fleet project. It taps an overlooked B2B segment: fleet companies with hundreds of drivers and vehicles, who can use their idle capacity to earn extra income through Lalamove.

- **New web app** A brand-new fleet web app built from scratch within three months.
- **Driver app changes** Adjustments to the existing driver app to support fleet-managed drivers.
- **Internal tools** Updates to internal tooling so operations could onboard and manage fleets.
- **One PM, many teams** Under time pressure, I managed up to 20 engineers at once, plus stakeholders across HQ and local markets.

## Results

### A product line that kept growing after launch.

- **Expansion** A year after launch, Fleet was still growing and being introduced into new markets.
- **Share of volume** Over 13% of 4-wheel transaction volume, with steady 5% month-on-month growth in 5 key markets.

## Learned

### Building something new inside something established.

- **Stakeholders** A new product inside an established company means balancing many goals and ideas, with pressure from the top to launch fast. Taking Fleet from idea to launch in my first few months was tough — and an incredible learning experience.
- **Process** Being part of a mature product team showed me what a good product and sprint process looks like — knowledge I carried into my next role.

## Problem

### A big early community that wasn't sticking around.

Atma's goal is to build the largest, most engaged community of lower- and middle-income job seekers, around one shared mission: to get better — a better job, better income, a better life.

When I joined, the product was early, with improvements needed everywhere. Atma had built a sizable community quickly, but struggled to retain it.

## Solution

### Rebuilding the product around engagement and retention.

I worked with the founders, designer and engineers to develop Atma from the ground up.

- **Atma 2.0 matching** Removed the barrier between job seekers and providers, letting them chat as soon as the algorithm matched them.
- **Academy** A quiz-based learning module for job seekers, built to lift daily retention.
- **Onboarding** Chat-based, dating-app style profile creation for better matchmaking.
- **Result automation** Qualified seekers automatically invited to interviews; others notified, instead of left waiting in chat.
- **Feed** An experiment into the social side of the app, connecting job seekers with each other.
- **Job platform & web** Providers list jobs shown in the app and on the web; a revamped provider site, SEO job pages and browsable company profiles.

## Results

### Growth on every metric that mattered.

- **Activation** Onboarding conversion rose above 80% after a few iterations.
- **Retention** Over 40% after the Academy release.
- **Scale** Daily active users grew from 1k to over 10k in a few months.
- **Speed** Atma 2.0 shipped in one month, from ideation to release.

## Learned

### Build swiftly, adjust quickly.

- **From scratch** Working closely with the founders, I helped set every layer of the product — vision, strategy, roadmap — from zero.
- **Pivots are normal** At an early-stage company, redirections are inevitable. It taught me to build fast without getting attached.
